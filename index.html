THE DETAILS

Before you
begin.

What does Sevren keep?+

No email, no IP logs, and no stored prompts or completions. Account balances and content-free usage totals are retained. Payments are public and linked to the account they credit. The network provider and upstream inference provider see traffic in transit. Read the full privacy boundaries.

How do I add credit?+

Pay ETH or USDG on Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663) from your account page. Credit appears after 900 block confirmations, usually 1 to 2 minutes later. USDG is credited one to one; ETH uses the Chainlink ETH/USD price. Credit cannot be withdrawn or refunded.

What do token holders get?+

From 0.5% of token supply, OUTLAW grants daily free tokens on paid models. From 1%, LEGEND grants higher allowances. A wallet holding at least 0.15% can also qualify for a one-time free try. Availability and amounts come from the live catalog. See holder benefits for current terms.

Can I use my existing agent?+

Yes, if it supports OpenAI Chat Completions or Anthropic Messages. Use your Sevren API key and the model ID from the catalog. Streaming and function tools are supported; image inputs are not. See the integration guides.
